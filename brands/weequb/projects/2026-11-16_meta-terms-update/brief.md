# Brief

**Brand:** Weequb Technologies (BRAND.md colours/logo still DEMO)
**Topic / goal:** Explain Meta's paused business-terms update (website crawling) to small-business owners and position Weequb as the website partner → comments
**Audience:** Tamil Nadu business owners who sell on Facebook/Instagram and have (or need) a website
**Language:** Tanglish: VO in Tamil script with English words, captions romanised
**Target length:** ~20 s
**Style reference:** knowledge/references/premium-elements/style.md, locked. Built with `templates/premium-motion/` (hook → icon cards → logo + CTA).
**CTA:** Comment "WEBSITE" (free website check)
**Fact check (as of 16 Nov 2026):** Meta planned updated Commercial, Product Catalog, Self-Serve Ad and Ad Creative Generative AI terms for 20 Nov 2026. The Commercial Terms would have allowed Meta to crawl business websites. On 5 Nov Meta paused all four; the existing terms stay in effect, and no new date has been given. Re-check Meta's Business Help Center before posting.
**Must avoid:** saying Meta is crawling sites now, or that it *will* return on a set date; legal advice
